Who We Are

Japan Quality. Global Trust.

Established in 2007 and headquartered in Chiba, Japan, N.S. Corporation is a trusted exporter of premium Japanese vehicles. For nearly two decades we have connected buyers across continents with Japan's finest automobiles — from luxury sedans and SUVs to hybrids and commercial vehicles.

What began as a dedicated family operation has grown into a global bridge between Japan's legendary automotive quality and drivers worldwide. We hold direct access to Japan's major auto auctions, allowing us to hand‑pick every vehicle against its auction sheet and grade.

Our promise is simple — honest deals, clear communication and full support, from the moment you inquire to the moment your vehicle arrives at your port.

— N.S. Corporation, Chiba, Japan
